Booking

You’ll see a confirmation page with your booking reference as soon as payment goes through, and we email you a confirmation within a few minutes.

If the email hasn’t arrived after an hour, check your spam folder, then find your booking with your reference and email address.

Signed in, go to My bookings. Without an account, use Manage my booking with your booking reference and the email address you booked with.

No. You can book as a guest. An account lets you see all your trips in one place, save travellers’ details for next time and keep favourites.

Names must match each traveller’s passport exactly. Contact us as soon as possible: small spelling corrections are often free, but some airlines charge for name changes or don’t allow them.

Yes. Prices include taxes and our fees. Optional extras such as luggage, seats, transfers or holidayfinder®Flex are added only if you choose them, and the total is always shown before you pay.

Payment

We accept Visa, Mastercard and American Express cards, PayPal, Apple Pay and Google Pay. The methods available for your trip are shown at checkout.

On many holidays, yes. You pay a deposit today and the balance is charged automatically to the same payment method on the date shown at checkout, usually 4 to 6 weeks before you travel.

You can pay in 3 monthly instalments at no extra cost when your departure is more than 12 weeks away. The first instalment is taken today and the next two are charged automatically to the same payment method.

We email you a reminder 3 days before each payment. See Payment options for the details.

Yes, in two ways. You can spread the total over up to 3 of your own cards, or share it with the other adults travelling: you pay your share and each of them pays theirs by link within 48 hours.

Yes. Card payments go through our payment provider’s secure page. We never see or store your full card number.

Changes and cancellations

Many bookings can be changed, depending on the airline’s and hotel’s rules. Open your trip in My bookings to see what you can change and what it costs, or contact us.

Open your trip in My bookings or Manage my booking. You’ll see what you get back before you confirm.

What you get back depends on the conditions you booked with. Our Cancellation policy explains how it works.

Refunds go back to the payment method you used. Once we have the money back from the airline or hotel, it usually takes 5 to 14 days to reach your account.

We’ll contact you by email and text with your options, usually a new flight or a full refund for the part affected. If your trip is protected, you won’t be left out of pocket.

holidayfinder®Flex

Flex lets you cancel for any reason, up to 24 hours before departure, and get 90% of your trip price back. It costs 10% of the trip price and is added at checkout. Read more about Flex.

Cancel in My bookings or with our 24/7 support team, up to 24 hours before your first flight (or check-in, for stays). The refund reaches your original payment method within 14 days.

No. Flex is a holidayfinder service. It doesn’t cover medical costs, lost luggage or delays, so we recommend travel insurance as well.

Baggage

It depends on the airline and the fare. Your baggage allowance is shown on each flight before you book and in your booking confirmation.

Usually, yes. Adding bags before you fly is almost always cheaper than paying at the airport. Open your trip in My bookings or contact us.

Most airlines carry pushchairs free of charge. Skis, golf clubs and bikes usually need to be booked in advance for a fee. Contact us before you travel and we’ll arrange it with the airline.

Transfers

Your transfer details, meeting point and the provider’s phone number are in your booking confirmation. Most drivers wait in the arrivals hall with a sign showing your name.

Your transfer provider tracks your flight and waits for you. If your flight number has changed, let us know so we can tell them.

Yes, as long as there is availability. Contact us at least 48 hours before you arrive.

Car rental

The main driver needs a full driving licence, a passport or ID card and a credit card in their name for the deposit. The exact requirements are shown on each car before you book.

The rental company blocks a deposit on the main driver’s card at pick-up and releases it after the car is returned. It can take up to 14 days to show as available again.

Usually, yes, at the rental desk. Additional drivers must show their own licence and may have to pay a daily fee.

Accessibility and special assistance

Tell us at least 48 hours before you fly and we’ll ask the airline to arrange assistance at both airports. It’s free of charge.

Yes. Airlines carry wheelchairs and mobility aids free of charge. Let us know the type, size and weight, and for powered chairs the battery type, so the airline can prepare.

Contact us before you book and we’ll check with the hotel which rooms suit your needs. Our Accessibility statement explains how we make this website work for everyone.
