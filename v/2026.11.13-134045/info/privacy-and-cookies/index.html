Who controls your data

holidayfinder, the company named at the foot of every page, is the controller of the personal data described here. Airlines, hotels and other suppliers also become controllers of the data we pass to them to provide your trip.

If you have a question about this notice or your data, contact our data protection team through Contact us.

What we collect

  • Contact details: name, email address, phone number and address of the person booking.
  • Traveller details: names, dates of birth, gender and, where the airline or destination requires it, passport or ID details.
  • Booking details: what you booked, when, how much you paid and your payment plan.
  • Payment details: the payment method and the last four digits of a card. Full card numbers are entered on our payment provider’s secure page; we never see or store them.
  • Special category data: health or mobility information, only when you give it to us so we can arrange assistance.
  • Usage data: searches, favourites, the device and browser you use, and cookies (see below).
  • Messages: what you send us by WhatsApp, phone or email.

How we use your data

We only use your data when the law gives us a reason (a lawful basis).

Who we share it with

  • Suppliers that provide your trip: airlines, hotels, transfer and car rental companies, event organisers and cruise lines.
  • Our payment provider, which processes your payment on its secure page.
  • Airlines and border authorities: airlines must send passenger information (Advance Passenger Information) to the authorities of the countries you fly to and from.
  • Service providers that host our systems, send our emails and help us with customer support, under contracts that protect your data.
  • Authorities such as the police, when the law requires it.

We never sell your personal data.

International transfers

When you travel abroad, your data has to go to suppliers in your destination, which may be outside the UK. Where our own service providers process data outside the UK, we use safeguards approved under UK data protection law, such as the international data transfer agreement.

Your rights

Under UK data protection law (UK GDPR), you have the right to:

  • get a copy of the personal data we hold about you;
  • have inaccurate data corrected — you can update most of it yourself in your profile;
  • have your data deleted, where we no longer need to keep it;
  • restrict or object to how we use it, including for direct marketing;
  • receive your data in a portable format;
  • withdraw your consent at any time, where we rely on it.

To use any of these rights, contact us through Contact us. We reply within one month.

Marketing

We only send you deals and travel inspiration if you have signed up or booked with us before. Every email has an unsubscribe link, and you can change your choices in your account at any time. Service messages about your booking are not marketing and are always sent.

Cookies

Cookies are small files saved by your browser. We use them to make the site work and, if you agree, to measure and improve it.

AnalyticsTells us how the site is used, so we can improve itYes

Changing your cookie choices

When you first visit, the cookie banner lets you accept all cookies, reject all optional cookies or choose by category. You can change your mind at any time from the cookie preferences, or by clearing cookies in your browser settings.

Children

Our services are not for children to use on their own. We only collect children’s details from the adult who books, to arrange their travel.

Security

We protect your data with encryption, access controls and regular security testing. Payments are handled by our payment provider on its own secure page, so your card number never passes through our systems.

Complaints

If you are unhappy with how we have used your data, please tell us first so we can put it right. You also have the right to complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office (ICO), the UK’s data protection regulator.

Changes to this notice

We update this notice when the way we use data changes. The date at the top shows the latest version. If a change matters to you, we will tell you by email.
