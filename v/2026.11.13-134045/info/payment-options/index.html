Pay in full

Pay the whole price today and there’s nothing more to think about. You can use any of the payment methods we accept.

Pay a deposit

Secure your holiday with a deposit today. The balance is charged automatically to the same payment method on the date shown at checkout, usually 4 to 6 weeks before you travel.

The deposit amount and the balance date are shown at checkout and in your booking confirmation.

Pay in 3 instalments

Spread the cost over 3 monthly payments with no interest and no fees.

  • The first instalment is taken today, the second and third on the same day of the following two months.
  • We charge the same payment method automatically on each date.
  • We email you a reminder 3 days before each payment.
  • Available when your departure is more than 12 weeks away, so everything is paid well before you travel.

Split the payment

Travelling with friends or family? Split the cost in one of two ways.

Between your own cards

Pay the total today with up to 3 of your own cards, and choose how much goes on each. This option takes cards only.

With the others travelling

Pay your share now and we send a payment link to the other adults on the booking. Each person pays their own share on their own device within 48 hours.

If someone doesn’t pay their share within 48 hours, we let you know so you can pay it yourself or change the booking.

Payment methods we accept

  • Credit and debit cards: Visa, Mastercard and American Express
  • PayPal
  • Apple Pay
  • Google Pay

Apple Pay and Google Pay are offered on devices that support them. You can save cards for next time in Payment methods.

Paying securely

Card payments are made on our payment provider’s secure, encrypted page. We never see or store your full card number.

Your bank may ask you to confirm the payment in its app or with a code. This extra check protects you against fraud.

Tip

Paying by credit card can give you extra protection from your card provider. Ask your bank for details.
