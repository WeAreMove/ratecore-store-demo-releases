holidayfinder®Flex

Plans change. With holidayfinder®Flex you can cancel for any reason up to 24 hours before you leave and get 90% of your trip price back.

Updated 28 October 2026

What Flex is

Flex is an option you add at checkout. It costs 10% of the trip price and lets you cancel the whole booking for any reason.

Illness, work, a change of plans: you don’t need to give a reason or send any documents. If you cancel in time, we refund 90% of your trip price to the payment method you used.

What’s covered

  • Cancel for any reason, with no proof or documents needed.
  • Up to 24 hours before departure: before your first flight, or before check-in for stays.
  • 90% of your trip price back, refunded to your original payment method within 14 days of cancelling.
  • The whole booking, for everyone travelling.

How to cancel with Flex

  1. 11. Open your trip in My bookings, or find your booking with your reference and email address.
  2. 22. Choose Cancel booking. You’ll see the amount you get back before you confirm.
  3. 33. Confirm the cancellation. We email you straight away.
  4. 44. Your refund reaches your original payment method within 14 days.

You can also cancel with our 24/7 support team. See Contact us.

What isn’t covered

  • The Flex fee itself and the remaining 10% of the trip price are not refunded.
  • Flex cancels the whole booking. Single travellers can’t be removed with Flex.
  • Cancellations less than 24 hours before departure or check-in.

Flex is not travel insurance

Flex doesn’t cover medical costs, lost luggage or delays. We recommend you take out travel insurance as well.
