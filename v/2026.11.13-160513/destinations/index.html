Destination guides

Where to go, when to go and what to do when you get there: honest, practical guides to our most popular holiday destinations, written and checked by our travel team.

12 guides

  • Tenerife

    Spain’s highest peak, black-sand coves and sunshine all year, with laurel forests and whales offshore.

    Discover Tenerife

  • Algarve

    Golden cliffs, sea caves and over 150 beaches on Portugal’s sunniest coast, with fresh grilled fish.

    Discover Algarve

  • Santorini

    Whitewashed villages on a volcanic cliff edge, caldera sunsets and crisp Assyrtiko wine.

    Discover Santorini

  • Lisbon

    Seven hills, yellow trams and riverside sunsets, with tiled old quarters and Atlantic beaches nearby.

    Discover Lisbon

  • Gran Canaria

    Golden dunes, mountain villages below Roque Nublo and a capital with a city beach: a continent in miniature.

    Discover Gran Canaria

  • Antalya

    Turquoise bays below the Taurus Mountains, a Roman harbour old town and the ancient theatre of Aspendos.

    Discover Antalya

  • Lanzarote

    Fire mountains, black-sand beaches and César Manrique’s art in lava caves on a striking volcanic island.

    Discover Lanzarote

  • Malta

    Honey-coloured fortress cities, 7,000 years of history and clear blue lagoons on sunny, compact islands.

    Discover Malta

  • Rhodes

    A medieval walled old town, the clifftop acropolis of Lindos and long sandy beaches in Greek sunshine.

    Discover Rhodes

  • Fuerteventura

    Endless white-sand beaches, Sahara-like dunes at Corralejo and steady winds for kitesurfing.

    Discover Fuerteventura

  • Mallorca

    Hidden coves, the mountain villages of the Tramuntana and Palma’s Gothic cathedral, with superb food.

    Discover Mallorca

  • Crete

    Minoan palaces, the pink sands of Elafonisi and the Samaria Gorge on Greece’s largest island.

    Discover Crete
