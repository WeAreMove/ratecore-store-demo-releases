Affiliate programme

Earn a commission when your readers and followers book holidays and stays with holidayfinder.

Updated 22 October 2026

Who it's for

Anyone who inspires people to travel and wants to recommend a store they trust.

  • Travel bloggers and writers
  • Creators on Instagram, TikTok and YouTube
  • Publishers, newsletters and comparison sites
  • Communities and clubs that plan trips together

How it works

  1. 11. Apply to join. We review every application, usually within a few working days.
  2. 22. Once approved, create tracking links to any page of the store.
  3. 33. Share them in your articles, videos, posts or newsletters.
  4. 44. When someone books through your link and completes their trip, you earn a commission.
  5. 55. See clicks, bookings and earnings in your dashboard, and get paid every month.

What you earn

A commission on completed stays and holidays; rates are in the programme terms.

Commission is paid on the value of bookings that are completed, not cancelled. Rates depend on the type of booking and are set out in the programme terms you accept when you join.

Tools for affiliates

  • Tracking links to any search, offer or page of the store.
  • Deep links to our [destination guides](/destinations), ready to recommend in your own travel content.
  • A deals feed with current prices and campaigns, refreshed every day.
  • Banners and photos you are allowed to use, in the sizes you need.

Programme rules

A few rules keep the programme fair for everyone.

  • No bidding on the holidayfinder brand name in search ads.
  • Always tell your audience that you earn a commission, as advertising rules require.
  • No misleading prices or claims: show prices as the store shows them.
  • No sending spam or buying traffic from sites we haven't approved.

How to apply

Email partners@holidayfinder.example with a link to your site or channels, the size of your audience and where they are based. We'll reply with next steps.
