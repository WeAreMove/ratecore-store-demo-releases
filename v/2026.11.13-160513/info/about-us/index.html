About us

holidayfinder is one place to plan and book your whole trip — package holidays, stays, flights, events, ski, cruises and car rental — with real people on hand around the clock.

Updated 28 October 2026

Who we are

We are a travel company built by people who love travelling and are tired of booking it in ten different tabs.

holidayfinder brings together the parts of a trip that usually live on separate sites. You can book a flight and hotel as one package, add a transfer and a hire car, find tickets for a match or a show, and keep everything in one booking with one reference.

Our team includes travel specialists, customer support agents, designers and engineers. Many of us have worked in travel for years — in hotels, airlines, tour operators and booking sites — and we bring that experience to every page of the store.

How we choose hotels and partners

We only sell what we would be happy to book ourselves.

We work with established airlines, hotel groups, independent hotels, cruise lines, car rental companies and local experience providers. Before a partner appears in the store, we check that they are properly licensed, that their prices and policies are clear, and that guests are happy with them.

  • Clear prices. Partners must give us the full price, including taxes and mandatory fees, so the price you see is the price you pay.
  • Honest descriptions. Photos, facilities and room details come from the partner and are checked against guest feedback.
  • Reviews from real guests. Only people who have stayed can review a hotel, and we show the low scores as well as the high ones.
  • Ongoing checks. If a partner repeatedly lets guests down, we stop selling them.

How we use technology

Our tools suggest; you decide.

AI Match looks at your dates, budget, who is travelling and the kind of holiday you want, and suggests destinations and offers that fit. It explains why each one was suggested, so you can see what it took into account. It never books anything for you, and the prices it shows are the same prices everyone else sees.

Our destination guides are written and checked by people, with the best time to go, what to do and where to stay, so you can make up your own mind before you search.
