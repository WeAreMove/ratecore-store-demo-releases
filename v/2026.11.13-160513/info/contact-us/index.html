Before you contact us

Many questions are answered in our Help Centre. You can also see, change or cancel your trip yourself in Manage my booking.

Have your booking reference ready

It’s in your confirmation email and starts with HF. It helps us find your trip straight away.

Help during your trip

If something goes wrong while you’re away — a missed transfer, a problem with your room or a cancelled flight — contact us straight away by WhatsApp or phone, any time of day or night.

In a medical or other emergency, call the local emergency number first (112 across the EU), then your travel insurer.

Making a complaint

We’re sorry if something wasn’t right. Telling us quickly gives us the best chance of putting it right.

  1. 11. If you’re still on holiday, tell us and the hotel or supplier straight away so we can try to fix it there and then.
  2. 22. If you’re home, email us within 28 days of your return with your booking reference, what happened and any photos or receipts.
  3. 33. We acknowledge your complaint within 2 working days and aim to give you a full answer within 28 days.
  4. 44. If you’re not happy with our answer, we’ll tell you how to take it to an independent dispute resolution service.
