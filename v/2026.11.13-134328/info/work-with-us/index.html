Life at holidayfinder

We help people spend their time off well, and we try to do the same for each other.

We are a team of travel specialists, engineers, designers and marketers who care about getting the details right — a clear price, a fast page, a helpful answer at three in the morning. We value curiosity, kindness and doing what we say we will do.

How we work

  • Remote-friendly. Most roles can be done from home, with time together in person a few times a year.
  • Small product teams. Each team owns a part of the store from idea to launch, and talks to travellers directly.
  • Time to travel. We encourage everyone to take their holiday and to see the places we sell.
  • Learning. A personal learning budget and time to use it.

Our teams

  • Engineering — the store, the apps and the systems behind every booking.
  • Design — product design, research and content design.
  • Travel experts and support — the people who help travellers 24 hours a day.
  • Partnerships — airlines, hotels, cruise lines and local experiences.
  • Marketing — campaigns, content, destination guides and our social channels.

Open roles

No roles listed right now

We don't have open roles at the moment, but we are always happy to hear from great people.

Send a speculative application to careers@holidayfinder.example with your CV or portfolio and a few lines about the team you'd like to join. We keep applications for 12 months and get in touch when a suitable role opens.

Our hiring process

Usually three to four weeks from application to offer.

  1. 11. A short call with our recruiter about the role and what you're looking for.
  2. 22. A conversation with the hiring manager.
  3. 33. A practical task or case study, shared in advance, that takes no more than a few hours.
  4. 44. Meeting the team you'd work with.
  5. 55. An offer, with feedback whatever the outcome.

Inclusion and accessible interviews

We want our team to reflect the travellers we serve.

We welcome applications from everyone, whatever their background, age, disability, gender identity, sexual orientation, religion or ethnicity. Tell us about any adjustments you need — extra time, a different format, captions or a sign language interpreter — and we will arrange them. Asking will never affect your application.

Hotels and local experiences

Run a hotel, an apartment or a local experience? Sell it to holidayfinder travellers.

We work with independent hotels, hotel groups, tour and activity providers and event organisers. Email partners@holidayfinder.example with your property or experience, where it is and how you currently sell it, and our partnerships team will get back to you.
