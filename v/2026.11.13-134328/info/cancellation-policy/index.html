How to cancel a booking, what it costs and when you get your money back. The terms of your own offer always come first.

How to cancel

  1. 11. Open My bookings, or find your booking with your booking reference and email address.
  2. 22. Choose the booking and select Cancel booking. You will see what you get back before you confirm.
  3. 33. Confirm the cancellation. We email you straight away with the refund amount and the date to expect it.

Prefer to talk to someone? Our support team can cancel for you, 24/7 — see Contact us. A cancellation takes effect on the day we receive it.

Your offer’s terms always apply

Your offer’s own terms always apply: they’re shown on the offer and in your confirmation. Where they differ from this page, the offer’s terms are the ones that count.

Package holidays

For a flight and hotel booked together, the charge depends on how many days before departure you cancel.

If a flight in your package is non-refundable once booked, its cost may be charged in full at any time. The offer shows this before you book.

Hotels and stays

  • Free cancellation rates: cancel for free until the date and time shown on the offer and in your confirmation. After that, the hotel’s charge applies — usually the first night or the full stay.
  • Non-refundable rates: cheaper, but you don’t get money back if you cancel or don’t arrive.
  • Partly refundable rates: the offer shows how much you get back and until when.

Flights

Flights follow the airline’s fare rules, shown on the offer under Flight terms. Many fares are non-refundable; some allow changes for a fee. If you cancel a non-refundable flight, you can usually still claim back the airport taxes — ask our support team.

Cars, events and cruises

  • Car rental: most rentals can be cancelled for free up to 48 hours before pick-up. The rental terms on each offer say exactly when.
  • Event tickets: tickets are usually non-refundable unless the event is cancelled. If it is, you get a refund for the tickets.
  • Cruises: cancellation is free at first. The deposit becomes non-refundable closer to departure and further charges rise up to departure, as shown in the cruise’s terms.

If you added Flex

With holidayfinder®Flex, you can cancel the whole booking for any reason up to 24 hours before departure and get 90% of the trip price back. The Flex fee and the remaining 10% are not refunded. Flex cancels the booking for everyone travelling.

If we or a supplier cancel

If we cancel your booking, or a supplier cancels a service, we will tell you as soon as possible and offer an alternative where we can. If you don’t accept it, you get a full refund within 14 days. Your rights for packages are set out in our Terms of sale.
