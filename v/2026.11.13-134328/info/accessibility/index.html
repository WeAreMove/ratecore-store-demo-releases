Accessibility statement

We want everyone to be able to plan and book a trip with holidayfinder. This statement explains how accessible our website and app are, and how to get help.

Our commitment

We aim to meet the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2 at level AA on every page of our website and app. Accessibility is part of how we design and build every feature, not a check at the end.

What you can do

You should be able to:

  • zoom in up to 400% without text spilling off the screen or content being lost;
  • search, book and manage your trip using only a keyboard;
  • use a screen reader such as VoiceOver, TalkBack or NVDA;
  • turn off animations: we respect your device’s reduce motion setting;
  • switch to dark mode, or follow your device’s setting;
  • read the site in right-to-left languages, with the layout mirrored.
AbilityNet has advice on making your device easier to use if you have a disability.

How we test

  • Automated checks with axe run on every page, in light and dark mode, before each release.
  • We complete key journeys — searching, booking, paying and managing a booking — with a keyboard only and with screen readers.
  • Colours are checked for contrast, including in each store’s own brand colours.
  • We check pages at phone, tablet and desktop sizes and at high zoom.

Known limitations

Some content comes from our suppliers and partners, and we can’t always make it fully accessible ourselves.

  • Some hotel descriptions and photos from suppliers don’t have text descriptions.
  • Some documents from suppliers, such as PDF vouchers and tickets, may not work well with screen readers.
  • Payment pages and wallets run by our payment provider, PayPal, Apple and Google follow their own accessibility standards.
  • Some maps are hard to use without sight; the same information is always given in text.

We are working with suppliers to fix these. If any of them stops you from booking, contact us and we will help you book another way.

Help with your trip

If you or someone travelling with you needs assistance at the airport, on the flight or at the hotel, let us know when you book or as soon as possible afterwards. We will pass your request to the airline, airport and hotel. See our Help Centre or contact us.
