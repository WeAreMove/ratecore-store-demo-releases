Why holidayfinder

Clear prices, flexible ways to pay, protection for your money and real people when you need them. Here is what you get when you book with us.

Updated 30 October 2026

The price you see is the price you pay

Every price includes taxes and mandatory fees.

We show the total for everyone travelling, from the first search to the payment page. Extras such as luggage, transfers or Flex are only added when you choose them, and you see the new total before you pay.

Pay the way that suits you

Pay in full, pay a deposit, spread the cost or split it.

  • Pay a deposit today and the balance automatically before you travel.
  • Pay in 3 monthly instalments with no interest and no fees, when you book far enough ahead.
  • Split the payment across up to 3 of your cards, or share it with the people you are travelling with.

See every option on Payment options.

Change of plans? Add Flex

Cancel for any reason up to 24 hours before you travel and get 90% of your trip back.

holidayfinder®Flex costs 10% of the trip price and needs no proof or documents. Read how it works on holidayfinder®Flex.

Your money is protected

Package holidays are financially protected, so you are covered if something goes wrong with a supplier.

Read what is protected, and how, on Book with confidence.

Real people, 24 hours a day

Message us on WhatsApp, call or email at any time — before you book, while you are away and after you are home.

Our support team are travel specialists, not a script. Find every way to reach us on Contact us.

Everything in one place

Flights, hotels, transfers, car rental, ski passes and event tickets in one booking.

One reference, one payment and one place to manage your booking. Save ideas to your favourites and share a list with the people you are travelling with.

Reviews you can trust

Hotel reviews come from verified guests who stayed there.

We publish good and bad reviews alike, and we never edit what guests write except to remove personal details or offensive language.
