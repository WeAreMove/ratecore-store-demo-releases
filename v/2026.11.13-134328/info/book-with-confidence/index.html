Your money is protected

Package holidays booked with us are financially protected, so you will not lose your money or be left stranded if a supplier fails.

When you book a flight and hotel together as a package, you have the protection of the UK's package travel rules. If we or a supplier in your package can no longer provide your holiday, you are entitled to a refund, or to a replacement holiday or to get home if you are already away.

Flight-inclusive packages are also protected under our ATOL licence. Your confirmation shows the ATOL Certificate that applies to your booking and explains what it covers. Keep it with your travel documents.

Check your confirmation

Your booking confirmation says which protection applies to each part of your trip. You can see it any time in Manage my booking.

What is protected and what isn't

Protection depends on what you book and how you book it.

Financial protection by type of booking
BookingProtection
Package holiday (flight and hotel)Protected under the package travel rules and, for flights, our ATOL licence
Ski holiday with flightsProtected as a package
Cruise with flightsProtected as a package
Hotel or apartment onlyNot a package; the hotel's own cancellation terms apply
Flight onlyNot a package; the airline's terms apply
Car rental onlyNot a package; the supplier's terms apply
Event tickets onlyNot a package; the organiser's terms apply

Financial protection covers supplier failure. It is not travel insurance: it does not cover illness, lost luggage or delays. We recommend that everyone travelling has suitable travel insurance.

Secure payment

Your card details are handled by our payment provider on a secure page and never stored by us.

  • Payments are encrypted and processed by a certified payment provider.
  • Your bank may ask you to confirm the payment in its app (Strong Customer Authentication).
  • Pay by card, PayPal, Apple Pay or Google Pay — see Payment options.
  • We will never ask for your full card number by phone, email or WhatsApp.

Free cancellation on selected hotels

Many hotels let you cancel for free until a date shown before you book.

Look for "Free cancellation" on the room or rate. The exact date and time are shown on the offer and in your confirmation. After that date the hotel's cancellation charges apply. Read more in our Cancellation policy.

Flex: cancel for any reason

Add holidayfinder®Flex when you book and cancel for any reason up to 24 hours before you travel.

Flex costs 10% of the trip price. If you cancel, you get 90% of your trip back to your original payment method within 14 days. No proof or documents needed. See holidayfinder®Flex.
